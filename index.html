Implementation Roadmap

How to build it: Proof to Scale

A progressive 3-phase rollout blueprint minimizing risk and proving customer ROI from day one.

Months 1–3

Phase 1: Pilot & Validate

  • Frictionless QR PWA web app (zero app store barrier)
  • Personalized Rider & Tyre Profile passport
  • Lightweight Mechanic mobile dashboard
  • Pilot in 1 test metro with 25 certified garages
Months 4–6

Phase 2: Engage & Retain

  • Automated WhatsApp Business & SMS notifications
  • Gamified points engine & rewards catalogue
  • Mechanic product certification courses
  • Peer referral bonus loop
Months 7+

Phase 3: Nationwide Scale

  • Full dealer ERP integration & stock data
  • Sales intelligence dashboard for Eurogrip leadership
  • Automated fraud detection on loyalty points
  • Multi-language support (Hindi, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali)

Suggested Modern Tech Stack

⚡ Front End (PWA)

Ultra-fast Next.js / React Progressive Web App. Instant launch from phone camera QR scan without app store installation friction.

🛡️ Secure Back End

Node.js microservices, PostgreSQL with connection pooling, OTP authentication via phone number, and Redis for rate-limiting.

💬 Omnichannel Messaging

Official WhatsApp Business Cloud API & transactional SMS webhooks for friendly, high-open-rate tyre service nudges.
